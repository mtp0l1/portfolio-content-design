Complaints for Super Instore and Darkstore

The supermarket's complaint trees, reorganized so buyers can describe the problem in a few steps.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

2026 · Argentina

Context

When something goes wrong with a supermarket order, the buyer opens a complaint and picks the problem from a tree of options. The trees were outdated: fields that no longer exist, no message when a complaint ends without compensation, and a different tone in each vertical. They will also become the rule base for the AI assistant that handles complaints.

Results

Instore and Darkstore trees updated, with the obsolete fields removed

Copy aligned with Food and ready to be the rule base for the AI assistant

What I did

Reviewed the Instore and Darkstore complaint trees and removed what no longer exists.

Aligned the copy with Food, in a tone that acknowledges the buyer without admitting fault.

Documented each path, like a product with a problem or a package that didn't arrive, and where each exit goes.

What changed

The buyer picks the problem in a few questions, with direct options like “Arrived damaged or open”.

A complaint that ends without compensation now gets a closing message, instead of a dead end.

People who need help say what happened and attach photos of the package and the product.

After sending, the screen offers to continue the conversation on WhatsApp.
