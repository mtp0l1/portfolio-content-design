Troca+, the incentive program for sellers

A redesign of the program that rewards partner store sellers for every phone trade-in.

Senior Product and Content DesignerTrocafone

Context

Troca+ is an incentive program for partner store sellers: when a customer trades in a phone, the seller gets a reward. After a year of monitoring and feedback, it was time to rethink the experience.

Results

Users valued the program, and the questions found guided Troca+ 2.0.

Post-launch tests with a satisfaction survey, interviews and a comparison of metrics.

What I did

Analyzed screen recordings and interviews to see what users did and where they got stuck.

Redesigned the dashboard and the copy for Troca+ 2.0.

Planned the post-launch tests: satisfaction survey, interviews and a comparison of metrics.

What changed

The dashboard shows the month status, the accumulated money and how the program works.

The screens answer the questions from the research: transaction status and statements from past months.

Areas that looked clickable but weren’t were reviewed.
