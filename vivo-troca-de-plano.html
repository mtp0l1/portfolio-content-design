Mobile plan change in the Vivo app

Changing a mobile plan right in the app, without being sent to the website.

Content DesignerVivo

Context

The data team found that plan change was among the topics with negative indicators. The journey at the time sent people to the e-commerce site, and churn was high. The question was whether we were offering the right plans. To investigate, I used design thinking methods.

Results

Plan change inside the app, without sending people to the website

The research showed that people didn't like the plans offered, which led to a new feature for the offers engine

What I did

With the team, analyzed 300 comments from Reclame Aqui, the call center, the App Store and social media, and benchmarked carriers and banks.

Built the CSD matrix, the empathy map and the use cases, and matched what people wanted against the business rules.

Co-created the prototype with the product designer: architecture, information hierarchy, accessibility, storytelling and content.

Planned the validation, with qualitative research, usability testing and card sorting with 8 people, and took what we learned into V2.

What changed

The change happens inside the app, with no hand-off to the website.

The screen opens with a recommendation in plain language, like “Get more internet for just R$ 65 more per month”, and shows the current plan as a reference.

Each plan shows its benefits in short bullets, and “See details” and “More offers” are one tap away.

Before confirming, the summary shows the total and the previous plan's penalties, with a notice about the first bill.

The final screen says the person will be contacted within 24 hours, by SMS, WhatsApp, email or phone.
