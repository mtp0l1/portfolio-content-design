Mobile plan switch without leaving the app

People switch plans in the app itself, with the offer that fits how they use it.

Content DesignerVivo

Context

Plan change was among the topics with negative indicators, and churn was high. The journey at the time sent people to the e-commerce site. The question was whether the problem was the journey or the plans offered.

Results

Plan change inside the app, with no hand-off to the website

Validation showed people didn't like the plans offered, which became a new feature for the offers engine

What I did

Discovery with 300 comments (Reclame Aqui, App Store, call center and social media), a benchmark of carriers and banks, a CSD matrix and an empathy map.

Matched what people wanted against the business rules and prioritized with How might we, Jobs to be done and a MoSCoW matrix.

Co-created the prototype with product design: architecture, information hierarchy, accessibility and storytelling.

Validated with qualitative research, usability testing and card sorting with 8 people, and took the findings into V2.

What changed

The change happens inside the app, with no hand-off to the website.

The screen opens with a recommendation in plain language (“Get more internet for just R$ 65 more per month”) and the current plan as a reference.

Benefits come in short bullets, with “See details” and “More offers” one tap away.

The summary shows the total and the previous plan's penalties before confirming, with a notice about the first bill.
