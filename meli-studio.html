Meli Studio: AI creation for image, video and audio

Mercado Libre teams create product and marketing assets with AI from templates, without starting from scratch.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

Meli Studio is Mercado Libre's internal AI creation tool. It brings together image, video and audio models so marketing and content teams can create product assets. Each media type had its own way to prompt, configure and review the output. People who weren't fluent in prompting got stuck on a blank screen, and adoption was the biggest challenge. The job was to listen to the people using the tool every day and turn what blocked them into interface improvements.

Results

One interface pattern for image, video, audio, styles, workflows and assets.

First-time users start from a template, not from a blank screen.

AI interface patterns documented and added to the design system.

What I did

Shadowed teams creating real assets, listened to what worked and what blocked them, and ran a UX audit of the creation flows.

Designed the quickstarts for first use: verified templates in Explore and the guided style mode.

Redesigned the create and edit flows with the design system and contributed the new components back to it.

Documented the interface patterns for AI: prompt bar, generating state, cancel and before and after comparison.

Prioritized with Product and Engineering in short cycles, based on usage data and user pain points, and defined adoption metrics for the redesigned flows.

What changed

Image, video and audio follow the same pattern: Create, Edit and Explore.

Explore opens with verified templates, each with a title, a description of what it delivers and a button to use it.

The prompt bar is the same across media, with style, model, format and quantity in view.

When editing, people describe the change in their own words and compare the original with the result, and can cancel while the image is generated.

Brand styles can be created through a guided step by step or directly in JSON, for those who already know the schema.

Ready-made workflows show the steps and the estimated cost before running.
