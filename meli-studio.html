Meli Studio: AI creation for image, video and audio

Mercado Libre teams create product and marketing assets with AI from templates, without starting from scratch.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

Meli Studio is Mercado Libre's internal AI workspace for image, video and voice, where marketing and content teams produce campaign assets. Adoption was the bottleneck: each media type worked differently, and non-technical creators froze on the blank prompt or quit after 4–5 failed generations. Sample text prompts didn't fix it, because outputs still missed brand guidelines. I shadowed creators in real sessions to turn their blockers into guided workflows, reusable styles and measurable adoption.

Results

First-session completion went from 2/8 (25%) to 6/8 (75%) in moderated task sessions.

Prompt effort fell from ~6 raw attempts to 2–3 guided steps to reach an on-brand asset, cutting time-to-first-value by more than half in task analysis.

~30% fewer wasted generations in test sessions with cost preview and mid-generation cancel. Async alerts for slow video renders are the next step.

1 unified AI pattern library across image, video and audio, reducing spec and handoff rework when plugging in new models.

What I did

Shadowed 8 marketing and content creators and audited the flows: most drop-offs happened before the first export, from prompt trial and error.

Tested V1 text presets, which failed brand consistency, and moved to verified templates and locked style schemas, in short cycles with Product and Engineering.

Redesigned Create and Edit with the design system and contributed AI patterns back to it: prompt bar, generating and cancel states, before/after split and cost preview.

Defined the adoption funnel with Product: template-to-export conversion, iterations per usable asset and 7-day return rate.

What changed

One Create, Edit and Explore pattern across image, video and audio, with model, style, aspect ratio and output count in a single prompt bar.

Verified templates instead of blank screens: Explore opens with tested presets showing expected output, steps and estimated credit cost.

Brand Styles in two modes: a guided builder for newcomers and direct JSON editing for power users who found the wizard too restrictive.

Natural-language editing: describe a change, compare original and result side by side, and cancel mid-generation to save credits.
