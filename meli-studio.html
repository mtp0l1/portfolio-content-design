Meli Studio: AI creation for image, video and audio

Mercado Libre teams create product and marketing assets with AI from templates, without starting from scratch.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

Meli Studio is Mercado Libre's internal AI workspace for image, video and audio. Its primary audience was the marketing team, who needed to produce campaign assets in-house to save on external audiovisual production costs. However, AI generation costs quickly became a bottleneck: non-technical creators faced a steep learning curve with prompting, burning API credits through trial and error. My role was to flatten this learning curve and turn non-deterministic AI outputs into consistent, brand-safe assets.

Results

First-session success jumped from 2/8 (25%) to 6/8 (75%) in the second round of moderated task sessions using the structured Prompt Library and templates, scaling further once AI Skills launched.

Cut prompt effort from ~6 raw attempts to 2-3 guided steps to reach an on-brand asset, sharply reducing wasted API generations and pre-production time.

Scalable scene continuity: marketing teams independently generated product videos and banners by swapping products inside validated scene templates, without needing advanced prompt engineering skills.

1 unified AI pattern library documented in the design system, speeding up engineering handoff as new models were integrated.

What I did

Qualitative research and testing (8 creators): I partnered with marketing peers to diagnose low adoption, finding that only 2 out of 8 creators could produce a usable asset in the initial unassisted flow.

Built a "Master Prompt" assistant via Deep Research: to move fast, I used Gemini Deep Research to compile credible prompt engineering frameworks and built a master assistant to categorize our initial Prompt Library by format.

Prototyped (Figma and Claude Code) and validated: I designed the UI with design system tokens, built interactive code prototypes, and validated the flows against real Marketing and Ads campaign briefs (such as Black Friday and product videos).

Tracked adoption and output quality: I aligned tracking around two core pillars: growing active creators (adoption) and increasing approved assets with minimal rework (quality and lower cost per minute).

What changed

From blank screens to AI Skills: we evolved the workflow in stages. We started with a categorized Prompt Library, moved to AI Assistants with brand knowledge bases, and culminated in automated AI Skills.

Verified templates and "Scene Tokens": beyond format presets (like 15s Reels), we built pre-validated base scenes (e.g., a 10s Meli box unboxing) where creators simply swap the product or character while preserving scene continuity.

Unified Create and Edit pattern: model, style, aspect ratio and output count remain visible in a single prompt bar across all media types, with both a guided mode and JSON editing.

Cost preview and cancellation: creators see estimated credit costs before running and can cancel mid-generation to avoid wasting company resources on wrong inputs.
