Meli Studio: AI creation for image, video and audio

Mercado Libre teams create product and marketing assets with AI from templates, without starting from scratch.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

Meli Studio is Mercado Libre's internal AI workspace for image, video and audio. The main target audience was the marketing team, who needed to produce assets quickly to save on external audiovisual costs. However, the cost of AI generation was becoming a problem. Non-technical creators struggled with prompting, leading to a high rate of wasted API credits through trial and error. My role was to reduce this learning curve and turn unpredictable AI outputs into consistent, brand-safe assets.

Results

First-session success jumped from 2/8 (25%) to 6/8 (75%) in moderated task sessions, after introducing the AI Skills and verified templates.

Reduced AI cost waste: I lowered the learning curve and cut down on prompt trial and error, directly reducing wasted generations and saving API credits.

Higher output quality: the marketing team was able to independently generate consistent, on-brand assets without advanced prompt engineering knowledge.

1 unified AI pattern library adopted across the tool, accelerating engineering handoff when plugging in new models.

What I did

Shadowed and tested: I started by researching 8 marketing creators and found that only 2 out of 8 could generate a usable asset without heavy friction.

Built a "Master Prompt" assistant: I used Gemini Deep Research to gather credible prompt engineering practices and built an internal assistant to help categorize and structure the initial Prompt Library.

Prototyped and validated: I created prototypes and ran task validations with the marketing and ads teams, using real campaign briefings, to make sure the AI Skills delivered consistent results.

Tracked adoption and quality: I shifted the focus from counting generations to tracking adoption (active users) and output quality (assets that matched expectations without endless rework).

What changed

From blank screens to AI Skills: we evolved the workflow in stages. We started with a structured Prompt Library, moved to AI Assistants with brand knowledge bases, and finally implemented automated AI Skills.

Unified Create and Edit pattern: model, style, aspect ratio and output count are visible in a single prompt bar across all media types.

Verified templates: creators start with pre-tested formats (for example, "15-second Reels") that automatically lock in the correct parameters.

Cost preview and cancellation: creators see the estimated cost before generating and can cancel mid-generation, to avoid wasting company resources on wrong inputs.
