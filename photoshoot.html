Photoshoot, phone photos without leaving the flow

A bridge between the computer and the phone to photograph the used device during the assessment.

Senior Product and Content DesignerTrocafone

Context

At partner stores, the used phone is assessed on a computer, but the device photos have to be taken with a phone. The biggest challenge was capturing the photos without people leaving the flow on the computer.

Results

-32% time on the photo step.

81% found the feature easy or extremely easy.

What I did

Mapped the flow on the computer and the phone to find where people dropped out.

Wrote the instructions for each photo and for the IMEI code.

Measured the impact with a user survey.

What changed

A QR code on the computer screen opens the camera on the phone.

The phone guides the steps: first the IMEI (*#06#), then the device photos, with illustrated instructions.

The photos return to the computer and the flow continues where it stopped.
