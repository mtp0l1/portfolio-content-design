Food benefit at Súper

Paying for groceries with the food benefit, right inside Mercado Libre.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

2025 · Brazil

Context

People who receive their food benefit through Mercado Pago can use it on Súper purchases. The opportunity was to grow the vertical's sales by showing the benefit on the store home and at payment. The first version is for Mercado Libre Brazil employees.

Results

3 points of the flow designed: store home, checkout and purchase detail

First version handed off to engineering, with use cases, specs and translations

What I did

Mapped where the benefit needs to show up, from the store home to the purchase detail.

Treated the benefit as a new payment method, with a balance and a “New” tag.

Documented the use cases, specs and translations.

What changed

The store home shows the benefit balance next to free shipping and delivery.

At checkout, the benefit appears among the recommended payment methods, with the balance.

After the purchase, the detail shows how much was paid with the benefit.
