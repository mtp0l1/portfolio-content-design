About

Strategy and hands-on work, in the same person

I’ve worked across e-commerce, fintech, telecom, health and logistics, at companies like Mercado Libre, Loggi, Banco do Brasil, Porto and Vivo. My background is in communications, and my work spans product design, content design and design engineering.

I designed conversational AI experiences before the LLM boom. Today I use large language models every day to research, structure, write and evaluate content, and to build design tooling with Figma, Google Apps Script and Vercel.

Skills: content strategy, content design, UX writing, content modeling, taxonomy, structured content, prompt design, AI-generated content, evaluation criteria and quality benchmarks, personalization logic, voice and tone, localization for Spanish and Portuguese, design systems, developer handoff, stakeholder management.
