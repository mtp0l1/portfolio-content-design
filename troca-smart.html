Troca Smart, from the street to the web

A landing page that takes a street campaign to a discount coupon on a phone trade-in.

Senior Product and Content DesignerTrocafone

Context

Troca Smart was a street campaign promoting the circular economy: buy a better phone by selling your old one. The touchpoint was a QR code on the ad, and people arrived on their phones.

Results

+15% funnel conversion.

-12% abandonment at checkout.

What I did

Wrote the copy for the landing page, the steps and the coupon confirmation.

Wrote the notices about the phone’s condition and the IMEI check, in plain language.

Aligned the page message with the street campaign.

What changed

The QR code on the street ad leads straight to a landing page built for the phone.

The page opens with the promise (“Save with the old, enjoy the new”) and a button to find a store.

The steps show how to use the old phone as a discount: assess, get the offer, pick the new one.

People get an R$ 100 coupon by email to use on the trade-in.
