Picker app for deliveries in minutes

Volume, stock, bags and proof of what was collected: four flow failures, four workstreams.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

Pronto App is the app used by the people who pick supermarket orders. With instant delivery, the picker flow showed four gaps: volume that doesn’t fit one courier, stock that triggers needless substitutions, bags with no count, and no proof of what was collected. Each one became its own handoff project.

Results

4 workstreams prioritized from real operational failures and documented for engineering

Volume alert (15+ items) and “Stock” tag with no extra steps: testing showed blocking modals slowed picking down, leading to inline visual cues instead

Scanning and tapping validated on the move: contrast, hierarchy, and touch-target adjustments after testing ensured fast alert recognition and accurate one-handed first taps

What I did

Led on-site discovery with operations (field observation and interviews with pickers and store leaders) combined with quantitative telemetry analysis of delivery errors to isolate the four critical flow failures.

Designed and wrote a dedicated screen or alert for each failure point, with its own handoff and no extra steps.

Validated the interface through usability testing, combining 5-Second Tests (quick alert scanning), First-Click Testing (first-tap accuracy), and one-handed mobile tests on the move.

What changed

A large order warns before collection, and the picker decides whether to call a second courier.

Low-stock items get a “Stock” tag. The picker offers a substitute instead of searching for more.

The picker confirms the bag count and hands the order to the driver at the end of picking.

At the store exit, the Customs screen shows what was collected, even if the supermarket’s system is down.
