Real-time tracking for deliveries in minutes

From prep to the door, buyers follow their order in the app, in push notifications and on the lock screen.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

Mercado Libre expanded Instant Delivery to Super, Food and Pharma. Tracking was the one built for traditional deliveries, designed for windows of hours or days. In a delivery that takes a few minutes, it showed neither the order's progress nor real-time events. Buyers were left wondering and anxious while waiting, and unprepared when the order arrived.

Results

Handoff ready for the 3 verticals, in Argentina and Brazil

The same status on every surface: app, push, Live Activity and home card

KPIs defined: fewer “Where is my order?” contacts and higher CSAT for Instant delivery

What I did

Structured the content from the brief: UX goals, KPIs and the concepts to communicate, in priority order.

Mapped every delivery state, from accepted order to receipt, and wrote the copy for each one across app, push, Live Activity and home.

Adapted tracking for Super, Food and Pharma, including what leaves the happy path: delay, product replacement, prescription review and cancellation.

Documented the handoff rules, like not showing a state when the courier arrives before the order is ready.

What changed

Buyers see the order on a map, with the courier and the time left.

The copy changes at each step: “The store is preparing your order”, “Arrives in 12 min” and “Hi, I'm here!”.

Close to arrival, the screen asks the person to get ready and shows the delivery code, which can be shared.

Push, Live Activity and home repeat the app status, with no mismatch.

After delivery, the Live Activity disappears in 2 minutes and a push asks for a delivery rating 15 minutes later.
