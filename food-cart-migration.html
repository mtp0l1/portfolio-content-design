Bringing Food to the marketplace cart

A food delivery cart moving into the marketplace cart, with a handoff of what to reuse and what to build.

Product DesignerMercado Libre

2026

Context

Food had its own cart and was migrating to the marketplace cart, which couldn't change. Keeping two carts was expensive, and Food missed out on marketplace improvements.

Results

12 of 17 cart use cases reuse an existing capability, and only 1 is new

Engineering used the handoff with no open questions, and it became a reference for another vertical

What I did

Split the cart into 17 use cases and aligned with product and engineering.

Gave each case one of four labels, like “capability” or “current”, to say what to build, reuse or leave as is.

Wrote a single rule for free shipping and minimum purchase.

What changed

Engineering knows, case by case, what to build, what to reuse and what to leave alone.

Only the minimum purchase decides whether the buyer continues. Free shipping only changes the shipping cost.

Identical kits now share one line with a quantity. It's the only new behavior.
