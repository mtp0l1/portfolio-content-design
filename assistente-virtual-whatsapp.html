Virtual assistant on WhatsApp

Intent disambiguation, a two-step fallback and context handoff, so the conversation doesn’t get stuck.

Senior UX WriterBanco do Brasil

Context

I planned the flows and content of Banco do Brasil’s assistant on the WhatsApp Business API, with Product, Business and IT, for urgent requests like balance, transfers, bill payments, limits and card blocking. When people wrote off-script or sent short messages like “my card isn’t going through”, the old flow fell into repeated errors or guessed the wrong intent in IBM Watson. The challenge was to unblock the conversation in self-service and hand off to an agent, with context, in cases of doubt or risk.

Results

From 3+ attempts to 1 direct choice to recover from ambiguous requests, with more accurate routing in IBM Watson

3 to 4 fewer repeated questions at the start of human service, thanks to the context summary on the agent’s screen

Faster implementation by IT and consistency across flows, with the standardized message library and tone of voice guide

What I did

I planned the dialogue flows, the rules for errors, ambiguity and missing context, and the human handoff with Product, Business and IT.

I trained the AI (IBM Watson) and analyzed real conversations in the logs to separate colliding intents (like “card blocked” after a wrong password and “I want to block my card” after a loss).

I wrote and documented the microcopy and message libraries of the Conversational Design System in Miro and Figma, to keep the tone of voice and speed up IT delivery.

What changed

When IBM Watson’s confidence falls in the middle range, the assistant confirms the intent with short options before asking for any data.

After the first comprehension failure, the assistant explains what it can do with a clear example; after a second failure in a row, it offers direct options or an agent.

In the handoff to a person (after failures or a fraud or theft risk), the agent gets the identified intent, the step where the conversation stopped and the data already validated.
