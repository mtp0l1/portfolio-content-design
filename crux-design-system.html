Crux, Trocafone’s design system

The components, tone of voice and documentation that scaled Trocafone’s design.

Senior Content Designer & OpsTrocafone

Context

Crux was created so Trocafone could build consistent, scalable and sustainable products. There was no single base of components, copy and documentation that Product, Marketing and CX could use.

Results

-40% rework in design reviews.

3 areas started using the system: Product, Marketing and CX.

What I did

Defined the tone of voice and the writing rules for components and messages.

Documented every component with usage, states and text specs.

Ran the system’s operation: review, maintenance and adoption by the teams.

What changed

A component library with fields, buttons, tags, menus and product cards, each with its states and error messages.

A color palette with scales and a layout grid for every team.

Trocafont, an icon family of its own, made for the product colors.

Text specs on the screens: style, size and tone for each level.
